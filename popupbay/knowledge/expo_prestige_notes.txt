EXPO PRESTIGETECHNICAL NOTES
Purpose: Organize the handwritten observations into a readable reference for planning, designing, and documenting Expo Prestige modular exhibition stands.
1. Structure and Configuration
Expo Prestige modular exhibition system is available in the following standard sizes/ configurations.
3 × 1 - W 780 x H 2367 MM
3 × 2 - W 1560 x H 2367 MM
3 × 3 - W 2340 x H 2367 MM
3 × 4 - W 3120 x H 2367 MM
The structure configuration should be planned according to the required booth size/ Floor plan
Booth/ space open sides configurations needs to be define -1, 2, 3, and 4-side open.
Obtain the required floor plan/layout or reference from the client.
Structure sizes and configuration / dimensions should be mentioned against the Top plan/ Layout.
2. Connectors and Structural Accessories
Connector options include straight connectors and curved connectors for different structural configurations.
Straight connectors – 190mm, 445mm & 780mm
90° inside & Outside Connectors: Used to create corner configurations such as L-shaped and U-shaped booths.
45° inside & Outside Connectors: Used to create 45° angled configurations.
Strut End Cap and Flat end caps are used to cover structure from the sides so that structure hardware will not be visible from sides.
Additional accessories may be required to properly close or finish open sections of the structure.
Stabilization foot are required, each structure required two foots. It’s a standard size.
Structure height is standards – 2367mm.
Magnetic Straight Strut are part of the system.
3. Graphics, Panels, and Branding
Digital vinyl print with lamination pasted on the PVC sheets and at the back side there is magnets.
Male/female Magnetic struts are used for attaching prints to the structure
Top branding panel sizes is standards – (1x1- W 780mm x H780mm and 1x2 – W 1560mm x H 780mm)
